# Value Proposition Canvas

## Part 1: Customer Profile

Our primary customer segment is **university students with elective flexibility who lack clarity on how courses connect to skills and careers**. This covers:

- Students without a course plan
- Students unfamiliar with Australian course system
- Dual-degree students
- Career-changers
- Final-year students trying to articulate skills

### Customer Jobs

#### Functional Jobs

- **Select courses each semester** - Students must decide which courses to enrol in, often under time pressure during enrolment windows.

- **Research course information before enrolling** - Students actively try to find out what a course actually involves (workload, assessment style, content, lecturer quality) but currently rely heavily on combinition of UQ website, Reddit, friends, and seniors.

- **Plan their degree pathway** - Students need to figure out course sequences, prerequisites, and how everything fits together across semesters toward graduation.

- **Build career-relevant skills** - Students are trying to develop the skills that relevant to their future career, but they cannot
consistently tell which courses contribute to this so far.

#### Social Jobs

- **Appear competent and prepared to employers** - Students want to present themselves as having a coherent skill set.

#### Emotional Jobs

- **Feel confident about their academic choices** - Students want reassurance they are on the right track.
