# Feature 2: Side-by-Side Course Comparison Tool

## Hypothesis

We believe that university students experience choice paralysis and confusion when faced with electives that sound identical or cover similar material. We propose a side-by-side comparison tool that explicitly highlights the differences in specific skills gained and career outcomes between two selected courses. This feature will reduce confusion and provide a practical, efficient way for students to make informed choices between similar options.

## Test

To verify this hypothesis, we will conduct 3 interviews per team member with university students. Interviews will focus on past experiences where students had to choose between similar courses, how they made their final decision, and their feedback on the concept of a direct comparison UI.

## Metrics

We will measure the following:
1. Frequency of the problem: How often students encounter courses that seem identical.
2. Perceived usefulness: Do students find a direct, side-by-side comparison valuable for course selection?
3. Priority factors: Which comparison points (skills, careers, difficulty, prerequisites) are most important when breaking a tie.
4. Behaviour change: Would this tool reduce the time and stress spent agonizing over electives?

## Criteria

We will consider the hypothesis validated if the following criteria are met:
- 70% or more students recall a time they struggled to choose between two very similar courses.
- 70% or more students say a side-by-side UI highlighting skills and careers would be highly valuable.
- 60% or more students would use this tool whenever they are stuck deciding between two electives.

## Interview Questions

1. **Have you ever had to choose between two electives that sounded basically the same on paper?**
   - What did you do to finally figure out the difference between them?
   - How confident were you in your final choice?
2. **When you are comparing two similar courses, what is the number one deciding factor that breaks the tie for you?**
   - Do you usually defer to which one is easier, or which one fits your timetable better?
3. **Imagine a tool where you can select two courses and view them side-by-side, specifically highlighting which distinct skills each one teaches. How would you use this?**
   - Would you expect it to also show which course is harder?
4. **If the comparison tool showed that Course A leads to "Software Engineering" and Course B leads to "Systems Architecture," how much would that help you decide?**
   - Is comparing career outcomes more or less helpful than comparing the specific coding languages taught?
5. **What would make a side-by-side comparison tool feel cluttered or overwhelming to you?**
   - How much information is *too much* information to see side-by-side?
6. **If you saw that Course A gave you 5 skills and Course B only gave you 2 skills, would you automatically pick Course A?**
   - Why or why not?
