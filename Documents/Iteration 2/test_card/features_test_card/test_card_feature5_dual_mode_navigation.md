
# Feature 5: Dual-Mode Entry Navigation (Explore vs. Validate)

## Hypothesis

We believe that university students select course  with fundamentally different mindsets depending on where they are in their degree. Students without a clear direction need to explore broadly, while students with a course already in mind need to validate it against their goals. We propose that offering two distinct entry modes,

- Explore ("help me find courses by skill or career")  
- Validate ("I have a course in mind, is it right for me?")
will significantly make SkillPath more relevant and usable for a wider range of students at different stages.

## Test

To verify this hypothesis, we will conduct 3 user interviews per team member with university students. Interviews will focus on how students currently approach course selection, whether they identify with an explore or validate mindset, and whether a split navigation concept feels natural and useful.

## Metrics

We will measure the following:

1. Mode identification: Do students clearly identify their current approach as exploration or validation?
2. Concept clarity: Do students find the two-mode concept intuitive and easy to understand?
3. Perceived usefulness: Would each mode meet the student's specific needs at their current stage?
4. Behaviour change: Would students actively choose a starting mode each time they use the tool?

## Criteria

We will consider the hypothesis validated if the following criteria are met
- 60% or more students clearly identify their current course selection behaviour as either exploration or validation.
- 70% or more students find the two-entry-point concept intuitive and easy to understand.
- 60% or more students say the relevant mode would meet their specific needs when selecting courses.

## Interview Questions

1. **Think about the last time you had to choose a new elective. Were you exploring a wide range of options, or were you checking whether one specific course was right for you?**
   - What information did you find yourself looking for in that situation?

2. **How clear are you on your career direction right now — do you feel like you know where you want to end up, or are you still figuring it out?**
   - How does that certainty or uncertainty affect how you choose courses each semester?

3. **Imagine a tool homepage with two large buttons. The first says: "Explore — help me find courses by skill or career." The second says: "Validate — I already have a course in mind, is it right for me?" Which would you tap first, and why?**
   - Do you think you would switch between the two modes regularly, or mostly stick to one?

4. **If you were in "Explore" mode with no specific course in mind, what would you most want to discover first?**
   - What would make that experience feel overwhelming or unhelpful rather than useful?

5. **If you were in "Validate" mode with a specific course already in mind, what are the top questions you would want the tool to answer about that course?**
   - Is there a piece of information that would cause you to decide against taking it?

6. **How do you generally feel about apps or websites that offer different starting paths depending on your goal — does that feel helpful, or does it add unnecessary friction?**
   - What would make the choice between the two modes feel natural rather than confusing?
