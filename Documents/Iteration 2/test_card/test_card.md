# Hypothesis

We believe that university students often select courses based on convenience factors (e.g., timetable fit, perceived difficulty, recommendations from friends) rather than focusing on the specific skills they aim to develop. We propose that a tool that shows how courses connect to skills and career paths will help students make more intentional, strategic course selections.


# Test

To verify this hypothesis, we will conduct **3-4 user interviews per team member** with university students. The focus will be on understanding how students currently make course selections and how they would respond to a **proposed tool** that maps courses to the skills they aim to develop and the career paths they may lead to.


# Metrics

We will measure the following:

1. **Student interest** in using a tool that links courses to skills and career paths.
2. **Perceived value** of the tool: Do students think it would help them make more **intentional** and **purposeful** course selections?
3. **Clarity**: Do students feel the tool makes it easier to understand the **skills** developed by each course?
4. **Behavior change**: Would students consider using the tool to **guide their course choices** in the future?


# Criteria

We will consider the hypothesis validated if the following criteria are met:

* **70% or more** students express **interest** in using a tool that maps courses to skills and career paths.
* **60% or more** students agree that the tool would help them make **more intentional** course selections.
* **50% or more** students say the tool would help them clearly understand the **skills** they are gaining from each course.


# Interview Questions

1. **Would you be interested in using a tool that shows how each course connects to specific skills and career paths?**

   * What would make this tool **appealing** to you?

2. **How helpful would it be for you to know exactly which skills you will develop from each course before you enroll?**

   * How would this influence your course selection?

3. **Would you use a tool that shows potential career paths based on the courses you take?**

   * How would this affect your course selection process?

4. **Do you think a tool that links your courses to your career goals would be useful?**

   * Would this help you align your courses with your future career?

5. **Would you use a tool to compare different courses based on the skills they develop?**

   * How would you prioritize features when choosing courses?

6. **How easy would it be for you to list the skills you have developed from each course you have taken so far?**

   * Would a tool that helps you articulate those skills be valuable?

7. **Do you feel confident that the courses you're taking now will lead you to your desired career?**

   * Would a tool that maps this out help you feel more confident?

8. **Think about a course you took recently that you were unsure about.**

   * How could a tool showing skills and career paths have helped you make a better decision?

9. **If this tool existed, would you use it every time you needed to choose a course?**

   * Would you recommend it to others?
