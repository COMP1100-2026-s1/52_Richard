# Part 1: Customer Profile

## Primary Customer Segment

- University students with elective flexibility who lack clarity on how courses connect to skills, future opportunities, and career outcomes.
- First-year students unfamiliar with university systems
- Students without a long-term course plan
- Dual-degree students
- Career changers
- International students unfamiliar with Australian systems
- Students who changed degrees after poor fit
- Final-year students preparing for employment

## Customer Jobs - Functional Jobs

- Choose courses each semester during enrolment periods
- Understand prerequisites and what courses unlock later
- Compare electives before enrolling
- Build useful career-relevant skills
- Understand what skills they already developed
- Plan a long-term degree pathway
- Recover from past poor course decisions or degree switches

## Customer Jobs - Social Jobs

- Appear organised and future-focused to family, peers, and employers
- Explain degree choices confidently
- Present strong skills in resumes and interviews

## Customer Jobs - Emotional Jobs

- Feel confident they are making the right decisions
- Reduce stress during enrolment
- Feel their degree has direction and purpose
- Avoid regret about wasted electives or switching degrees

## Customer Pains - Functional Pains

- University planners are confusing and not intuitive
- Course information is fragmented across multiple websites
- Hidden prerequisites make long-term planning difficult
- Students cannot easily compare electives
- Skill outcomes are vague or missing
- Students rely on ChatGPT, Reddit, friends, or guesswork

## Customer Pains - Social Pains

- Embarrassment when unable to explain what they learned
- Fear of appearing lost or behind peers

## Customer Pains - Emotional Pains

- Anxiety during enrolment periods
- Regret after taking subjects with low value
- Lack of confidence in current pathway
- Feeling lost in a broad degree

## Customer Gains - Required Gains

- Easy-to-use planning tool
- Clear prerequisite maps
- Transparent course information
- Skill outcomes shown before enrolment

## Customer Gains - Expected Gains

- Career pathway visibility
- Better confidence in course choices
- Faster decision-making during enrolment

## Customer Gains - Desired Gains

- Personalised recommendations
- Skill tracker across degree
- Resume-ready skill summaries
- Alerts for future prerequisite opportunities
- Confidence that current choices lead somewhere valuable

# Part 2: Value Map

## Products and Services

- Course-to-skill mapping engine
- Career pathway visualiser
- Semester planning dashboard
- Future prerequisite alert system
- Side-by-side course comparison tool
- Personalised recommendations
- Skill progress tracker
- Resume skill summary generator
- Peer insight / review layer

## Pain Relievers

| Pain                            | Pain Reliever                                                       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Confusing planners              | Clean, intuitive interface simpler than existing university systems |
| Hidden prerequisites            | Visual pathway maps show what current decisions unlock later        |
| Unclear career outcomes         | Career mapping shows how courses connect to industries and roles    |
| Cannot identify skills          | Course pages list specific transferable and technical skills        |
| Information overload            | Consolidates planners, reviews, and course data in one place        |
| Course regret                   | Compare future value before enrolling                               |
| Reliance on informal advice     | Structured peer insights replace scattered Reddit threads           |
| Degree switching after poor fit | Early pathway clarity reduces costly misalignment                   |
| Stress during enrolment         | Fast personalised suggestions reduce pressure                       |

## Gain Creators

- Intentional course decisions based on future goals
- Stronger confidence in academic direction
- Better employability through visible skills
- Long-term planning clarity
- Less wasted time and money from wrong course choices
- Easier articulation of strengths in job applications
- Ongoing dashboard showing progress toward career goals
