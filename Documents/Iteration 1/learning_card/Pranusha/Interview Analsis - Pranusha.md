# Interview Analysis - SkillPath

## User Profiles

| Interview # | Degree                                  | Year     | Key Context                                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1           | Computer Science (Cyber Security focus) | 1st Year | Chose courses mainly from a friend's recommendation and the UQ website. Confident in choices but unsure about correct course order.     |
| 2           | Engineering                             | 1st Year | Used research and the UQ website planning guide. Motivated by future career goals and salary considerations.                            |
| 3           | Engineering                             | 1st Year | Followed recommended core courses and advice from a senior friend. Focused on required maths and science subjects.                      |
| 4           | Unknown STEM program                    | 1st Year | Used the UQ website but found it somewhat frustrating to navigate. Strong career direction but struggled to articulate skills gained.   |
| 5           | Engineering                             | 1st Year | Used the UQ website and selected courses based on strengths in high‑school subjects. Not fully confident in long‑term career direction. |

## Pain Points & Frustrations

Theme: Lack of clarity about course order and timelines

"Maybe more like if the order I do them matters…" - Interviewee #1

"What the best timeline to do courses would be." - Interviewee #2

Theme: Difficulty understanding skills developed from courses

"I am so confused, I have no idea." - Interviewee #4

Students often mentioned general skills such as time management rather than specific technical skills.

Theme: Reliance on external guidance

"My friend who is in second year sent me links… this is what I should do." - Interviewee #1

"I have a friend in third year of engineering who recommended courses." - Interviewee #3

Theme: University websites are not fully user‑friendly

"I didn't like how it kept all in one tab and didn't open up new tabs." - Interviewee #4

Several students reported that the website was "kind of hard" or confusing.

## What Students Need

- Clear course timeline showing the recommended order of subjects to reach a career goal.
- A tool that connects student interests and skills to possible degree pathways.
- Better explanation of what skills each course develops.
- A user‑friendly platform that simplifies course planning.
- Comparison of multiple pathways or alternative course sequences.

## Unexpected Insights That Challenge Assumptions

- Many students are already somewhat confident in their choices because they rely on friends or the official planning guide.
- Students rarely choose courses purely because they are easy; most prioritise required courses for their degree.
- Students are more concerned about course order and pathways than about the content of individual courses.
