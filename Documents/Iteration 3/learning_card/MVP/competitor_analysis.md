# Competitor Analysis


| Competitor | Target Market | Key Value Propositions | Weaknesses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn Learning / LinkedIn** | University students, early-career professionals, working adults globally | • Vast course library (20,000+) • Skill badges visible on profile • Direct link to job listings • Employer recognition | • Not tailored to university degrees • No degree-course mapping • Passive learning, not path planning • Paid subscription required | Generic professional platform — not built for students navigating a degree. No integration with university systems. |
| **Handshake** | University students (US-focused), graduate job seekers, campus recruiters | • University-integrated job board • Career events & fairs • Employer partnerships • Resume building tools | • No skill tracking or visualisation • Limited to job search, not planning • US-centric, limited AU presence • No personalised degree pathway | Strong on job placement, weak on skill development journey. Doesn't address skill invisibility. |
| **My Career Match** (uni portals) | Enrolled university students, domestic undergraduates, career counsellors | • Career personality assessments • Course suggestions per uni • Free via student login • Integrated with enrolment | • Static, one-time assessments • No ongoing skill tracking • Doesn't capture co-curricular activity • Low student engagement | Exists but rarely used. Students report it feels generic and doesn't evolve with them over time. |
| **Folio / Portfolio Tools** | Creative & design students, portfolio-focused disciplines, early-career creatives | • Visual portfolio showcase • Project-based evidence • Shareable with employers • Clean UI for work samples | • No career path planning • Discipline-specific (mainly creative) • No skill gap analysis • Doesn't map to degree outcomes | Good for showcasing work but not for planning or understanding skills across a whole degree. |
| **Coursera / edX** | Self-directed learners, upskilling professionals, international students | • Recognised certificates • University-partnered courses • Structured learning paths • Free audit options | • No integration with enrolled degree • No personalised university pathway • Completion rates are low • Not gamified or motivating | Supplements learning but doesn't solve the core problem of mapping a student's existing university journey. |
| **Grad Ready / CareerSmart** | Final-year Australian students, graduate job seekers, university career services | • Graduate employability modules • Interview & resume prep • AU-focused content • Campus partnerships | • Activated too late (final year) • No early degree planning • No skill-tree visualisation • Content-heavy, not personalised | Addresses employability at the end but misses the opportunity to guide students throughout their degree. |
| **OUR MVP — SkillPath** | Year 1–3 university students (AU), students with unclear career direction, students with co-curricular experience | • Visual skill-tree across full degree • Maps courses + jobs + extracurriculars • Personalised career pathway • Gamified progression for engagement • Skill gap identification & recommendations | • New product — no brand trust yet • Relies on student self-input • Requires uni data partnerships • Limited to MVP feature set initially | Directly addresses skill invisibility and disconnected planning. The only solution combining degree mapping, co-curricular tracking, and gamified progression in one place. |
