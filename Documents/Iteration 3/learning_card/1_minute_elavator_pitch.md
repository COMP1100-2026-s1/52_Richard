Have you ever picked a course just because it fits your timetable - and regretted it?

We are Skillpath from group 52. \[Briefly introduce all the team members\]

**PROBLEM**

University students choose courses that shape their careers, but they're doing it blind. They use three separate tools to figure out the skills a course builds and how it connects to their goals. Our interviews found most of the students choose based on timetable or difficulty, not on the skills they want to develop.

**SOLUTION**

SkillPath is a course discovery platform that maps university courses to real skills and career outcomes, but all in one place. It provides features such as, search by skill, compare courses side-by-side, and visualise exactly where your study plan leads.

**UNIQUE VP**

Other tools tell you what a course covers but SkillPath tells you what you'll be able to _do_ after taking it. It focuses on skills and careers, not just course codes and course titles. The majority of our prototype testers said they would use SkillPath before enrolling, confirming it replaces the three separate tools they currently use.

**CALL TO ACTION**

Students should stop treating course selection as a gamble or guesswork. Before you enrol next semester, try SkillPath first.
