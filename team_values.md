##  Code of Conduct
 
| #  | Principle          | Description                                                        |
|----|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | **Accountability**  | Take ownership of your actions and mistakes                       |
| 2  | **Respect**         | Treat all team members with courtesy and dignity                  |
| 3  | **Participation**   | Actively engage in all group discussions and tasks                |
| 4  | **Compliance**      | Adhere to all University of Queensland rules and policies         |
| 5  | **Punctuality**     | Be on time to all scheduled meetings                              |
 
---
 
##  Member Obligations
 
Each team member agrees to the following obligations:
 
| #  | Obligation                                                                                          |
|----|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Attend all scheduled team meetings; if unable to attend, notify the team **at least 24 hours** in advance |
| 2  | Respond to team messages (Discord, email, or other agreed channels) within **24 hours** on weekdays |
| 3  | Complete all assigned tasks **by the agreed-upon deadline** or communicate blockers early            |
| 4  | Review and provide constructive feedback on teammates' work when requested                           |
| 5  | Keep the shared GitHub repository up to date with your contributions                                |
| 6  | Maintain academic integrity — never plagiarise, falsify contributions, or misrepresent work         |
| 7  | Declare any use of Generative AI tools in accordance with UQ's GenAI policy                         |
 
---
 
##  Duties & Responsibilities
 
| Duty                        | Description                                                                                     |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Task Ownership**          | Each member is responsible for the tasks assigned to them and must see them through to completion |
| **Quality Assurance**       | Test and review your own work before submitting or pushing to the repository                     |
| **Documentation**           | Maintain clear commit messages, code comments, and update project documentation as needed        |
| **Knowledge Sharing**       | Share useful resources, tutorials, or insights that may benefit the team                         |
| **Meeting Preparation**     | Come to meetings having reviewed the agenda and any relevant materials beforehand                 |
| **Conflict Escalation**     | If an issue cannot be resolved within the team, escalate to the course tutor or coordinator      |
 
---
 
##  Conditions & Consequences
 
### Expectations
 
| Condition                                                                                          |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| All members must contribute **equitably** to the project — workload should be distributed fairly    |
| All code must be pushed to the team's GitHub repository — no work should exist only on local machines |
| Meeting minutes must be recorded and shared after every team meeting                                |
| Any changes to deadlines or scope must be **agreed upon by all members** before taking effect       |
| Confidential project materials (e.g., interview data, user research) must not be shared externally  |
 
### Breach & Consequences
 
| Level      | Breach                                                        | Consequence                                                                                   |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ⚠️ Minor   | Late to a meeting without prior notice                        | Verbal reminder from the team                                                                 |
| ⚠️ Minor   | Missing a non-critical deadline by less than 24 hours         | Written acknowledgement and catch-up plan                                                     |
| 🔶 Moderate | Repeated failure to attend meetings or respond to messages    | Formal team discussion; documented warning                                                    |
| 🔶 Moderate | Submitting incomplete or low-effort work without communication | Reassignment of tasks; documented warning                                                     |
| 🔴 Major   | Plagiarism, academic misconduct, or falsifying contributions  | Immediate escalation to the course coordinator; reported under UQ Academic Integrity Policy    |
| 🔴 Major   | Persistent non-contribution after multiple warnings           | Escalation to the course coordinator; may result in individual grading                        |

##  Conflict Resolution Process
 
| Step | Action                                                                                      |
|------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | **Direct Discussion** — Raise the issue privately and respectfully with the individual      |
| 2    | **Team Discussion** — If unresolved, bring the issue to a full team meeting for discussion  |
| 3    | **Mediation** — If still unresolved, seek mediation from a tutor or course staff member     |
| 4    | **Formal Escalation** — As a last resort, escalate to the course coordinator                |
