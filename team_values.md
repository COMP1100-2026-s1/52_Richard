#  Team Charter — Code of Conduct

| #  | Rule                                                                 |
|----|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  |  **Accountability** — Take ownership of your actions and mistakes |
| 2  |  **Respect** — Treat all team members with courtesy and dignity    |
| 3  |  **Participation** — Actively engage in all group discussions and tasks |
| 4  |  **Compliance** — Adhere to all University of Queensland rules and policies |
| 5  | **Punctuality** — Be on time to all scheduled meetings            |
