Brian: Hello, my name is Brian. I’m doing a project for Software Innovation, and I’m researching how people choose courses. I’m going to ask around 5 to 10 questions, and this interview will be recorded. Are you fine with that?

Interviewee: I am fine with that.

Brian: Okay. Starting with the first question, how did you decide which courses to take this semester? For example, what is the main thing that affects your decision?

Interviewee: Honestly, I mainly chose my courses based on how relevant they are to my future career, especially in AI and machine learning. So I try to balance between core subjects that I have to take and electives that actually build useful skills.

Brian: Before you took these courses, was there anything you wish you had known beforehand?

Interviewee: I think I wish I had clearer information about how heavy each course actually is, not just the course description.

Brian: Can you walk me through the last time you picked an elective course?

Interviewee: Since this is my first semester here, I checked the course information online, looked at reviews from other students, and also asked some seniors what they thought about it.

Brian: Do you have any specific tools or platforms that you used to find information about the course?

Interviewee: There is a lot of information on the web. You can search the course number, for example something like ECON1000. You can also ask in forums, ask friends, and look at the course profile.

Brian: Are you a first-year student, or have you already finished one semester?

Interviewee: I am a first-year student.

Brian: And you have not finished a semester yet?

Interviewee: No, not yet.

Brian: Do you have a rough idea of what jobs or roles you want to do after graduating?

Interviewee: I’m aiming for something in AI or machine learning. I think my current courses are helping, but not fully enough. There is definitely a gap, especially in real-world experience and projects.

Brian: From your current courses, do you feel confident that they are leading you toward your expected job?

Interviewee: Probably not fully yet. Since I’m still in first year, I only have an early idea about that.

Brian: After picking those courses, is there any course that you want to unenrol from?

Interviewee: I do not regret taking them, but I do think some courses did not really help me grow. In the future, I would prefer to focus more on choosing courses based on skills, not just difficulty or convenience.

Brian: So from your personal experience right now, do you have any courses that you regret?

Interviewee: Not at the moment.

Brian: Since you have picked your first-semester courses, do you think you now have a new way of choosing courses for next semester, or will it stay the same?

Interviewee: Since I’m doing a double degree program, back in my old university I mostly cared about easy courses and fitting my schedule. But in the next semesters, I think I will care more about skills for my future career and whether the course actually adds value.

Brian: Okay, I think that’s all for this interview. Thank you very much. Do you have any questions?

Interviewee: No.

Brian: Okay, thanks.

Interviewee: Thank you. Bye-bye.
