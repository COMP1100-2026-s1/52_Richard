**Interviewer:** Good afternoon. I would like to interview you for my Software Innovation course. Do you mind if I record this interview?  
**Interviewee:** Yes, I don’t mind.

**Interviewer:** Starting off from our prototype, what do you think about the homepage? Was it easy or difficult to use?  
**Interviewee:** The homepage is very simple. There are only two options, so it was easy to understand and click on them.

**Interviewer:** For the second task, searching by skill or career, how was your experience?  
**Interviewee:** Could you repeat that?  
**Interviewer:** When you used the search feature to find courses related to a career or skill you want, how was the experience?  
**Interviewee:** It was very easy. I just needed to type the course or skill I wanted and the results appeared very quickly. It was very responsive.

**Interviewer:** When you opened any course in the prototype, were you able to see the reviews on each course card?  
**Interviewee:** Yes. I could open the course and go back easily. The peer review section was easy to access.

**Interviewer:** When you compared courses, was it easy or a hassle?  
**Interviewee:** For me, this was the hardest part at first, but I only needed about five seconds to figure it out. After that, it felt pretty easy.

**Interviewer:** How about understanding the future pathways generated by the product? Was it easy or insightful?  
**Interviewee:** I think it’s very useful. For example, if I want to become a data scientist, I can go to that page and see the skills needed and the courses that help me reach that goal.

**Interviewer:** Overall, do you think you would use this application?  
**Interviewee:** Probably, especially if all courses are included in the future. Right now I understand it’s still a prototype.

**Interviewer:** What was the most useful feature for you?  
**Interviewee:** The career pathways feature.

**Interviewer:** What was the most confusing feature?  
**Interviewee:** Nothing was very confusing, but the compare courses feature could be improved a little.

**Interviewer:** What aspects could be improved?  
**Interviewee:** Maybe add more images. Right now it’s mostly text, so visuals would make it more engaging.

**Interviewer:** That’s all for the interview. Do you have any questions about the prototype?  
**Interviewee:** What is this application for?  
**Interviewer:** It’s designed to help students plan their courses and connect them to their desired career path. For now, it focuses on UQ courses and will integrate with the UQ API.  
**Interviewee:** What is the “Saved Courses” feature?  
**Interviewer:** It’s a future feature. It will allow users to save courses and track them in their learning benchmark.

**Interviewer:** That’s all. Thank you very much for your time.  
**Interviewee:** Thank you.
