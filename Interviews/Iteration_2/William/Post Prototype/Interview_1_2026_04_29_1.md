**Me:** Hi, thank you for agreeing to do the prototype testing earlier. Since you have tested our prototype, we are now moving on to the final interview questions. First, I need to get your consent for this interview. Are you okay with being recorded?

**Interviewee:** Yes.

**Me:** Going back to the first question, what were your first thoughts when you saw the homepage?

**Interviewee:** Honestly, I was a bit confused about what I was supposed to choose. There were two options, like “Explore” and “Validate,” right? I think it was a bit confusing how you were supposed to start off.

**Me:** Okay, that’s fair. Moving on, how did you find moving around the platform?

**Interviewee:** I think it was pretty easy, but I do believe some parts of Skill Path were not intuitive, especially the course comparison part. I was a bit lost on how to compare courses. But other than that, it was pretty straightforward.

**Me:** How useful were the recommended courses, do you think?

**Interviewee:** I think they were pretty useful, at least for now as a prototype. Personally, the courses do not align with my major specifically, but if they did, I think it would be extremely useful in finding the courses that I need for my current path.

**Me:** How valuable do you think it is to be able to see each skill from each course? Do you think there is any skill information that should have been added?

**Interviewee:** I think it was really useful. I liked that it was brief and concise, so it was easy to see what skills I needed. Also, in the comparison part, I really liked how it compared the skills from different courses. I do not think anything needs to be added for that part.

**Me:** How helpful was the semester planner? Do you feel that it was useful, or not useful at all?

**Interviewee:** Was the semester planner the part where you are able to pick what electives you need to choose?

**Me:** I believe it is the suggestion part.

**Interviewee:** It was pretty useful, but since I kind of overlooked it, maybe it was not very noticeable.

**Me:** How about the career path feature? How useful was it in linking the course to your career? Do you feel that any other features should be added for the career part?

**Interviewee:** I think it was useful because it is definitely important to see whether the course you are going to take is aligned with your career goals. I definitely liked that feature because it helped me see the relevance to my studies.

**Me:** Would you see yourself using this tool every semester?

**Interviewee:** I definitely would. I remember when I had to choose a minor, I struggled a lot with choosing which course to take. I feel like Skill Path would have definitely helped me compare which course would be more valuable for me, or which course could give me better skills. So yeah, I would definitely use it.

**Me:** If you could change one thing in the UI, what do you think it would be?

**Interviewee:** I think the navigation. Some parts made me feel a bit lost, especially in terms of how to do certain things or how to use certain tools.

**Me:** Is it because the buttons were unclear?

**Interviewee:** I think specifically for the course comparison part, it would be easier to just click and compare. But there were multiple buttons that I had to click in order to compare two courses.

**Me:** Do you feel that any other features should be added, or do you feel that any features should be removed because they are not useful?

**Interviewee:** I think as of right now, it is already okay. I do not think anything needs to be added or removed because all of the information given about the course is pretty relevant.

**Me:** Alright, I think that’s it for the interview. Thank you so much for your time.

**Interviewee:** Thank you.
