**Me:** Can I get around 5 to 10 minutes of your time to interview you for my project?

**Interviewee:** Yes.

**Me:** I would like to inform you in advance that this interview will be recorded.

**Interviewee:** All right.

**Me:** I’ll start off by asking: would you be interested in using a tool that shows how each course connects with specific skills and career paths?

**Interviewee:** Yes, I would be interested in using such a tool.

**Me:** What do you think would make the app more appealing to use?

**Interviewee:** I think something that would make it appealing would be knowing what different skills are being offered after taking the course.

**Me:** How helpful would it be for you to know exactly which skills would be developed after each course before you enroll?

**Interviewee:** I think it would help me set the foundation and basis for what I will get to achieve out of the course. Instead of going in blindly, not knowing what I’m going to achieve or needing to Google what I’m going to learn from it, I would at least have a better idea of what I’m going to learn. Then, it can help with my decision-making on whether I want to select the course or not, with the help of this tool.

**Me:** If we added a feature that shows a potential career path based on the current courses that you have taken, how do you think this would affect your method of selecting your electives right now?

**Interviewee:** I think it would help in a way where I can know what skills are not being offered in the course that I’m taking, and then it can help identify the gaps. For example, if I did a course on the foundation of marketing and only learned the basics of marketing, but I want to further develop my skills, I can use the tool to select electives that help me better understand or learn more about the marketing sector.

**Me:** How easy would it be for you to list the skills that you have developed so far from every course that you have taken? You don’t have to list them all out, just in your opinion, how easy do you think it would be?

**Interviewee:** I wouldn’t be able to give an answer on how easy it is, but I guess it would be more like general skills. I don’t feel like they are specific skills that I can list down.

**Me:** Do you think a tool that helps you articulate those skills would be valuable?

**Interviewee:** Yes, I think so. As I mentioned earlier, I feel like the skills that I currently have, or the skills that I can think of, are very broad. Sometimes I want them to be a little bit more specific, and I feel like it can target niche areas if I want to go into something more niche, for example within the marketing sector.

**Me:** Do you feel confident that the courses you are taking now will lead to the career that you want to do in the future?

**Interviewee:** I think some of the courses that I am taking spark interest in me, where I feel like I want to do this long term. But there are also some courses where I feel like I do not want to do it ever in my life.

**Me:** Think about a course that you took recently as an elective, where you were not really sure whether it was a good choice or not. How could a tool showing skills and career paths have helped you make a better decision?

**Interviewee:** I think one of the courses that I took for fun was a health and diet course. I just took it because I didn’t know what to do. Having such a tool would allow me to know what other skills I would get out of the course. I think it would be beneficial, even though it might not be related to my degree itself. In the near future, I could know how I can use the skills developed from this elective in my life, not just specifically for my career path.

**Me:** That’s all my questions. Thank you for your time.

**Interviewee:** Thank you.
