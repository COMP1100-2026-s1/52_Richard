**Me:** Hi, could I get around 5 to 10 minutes of your time to interview you for my project?

**Interviewee:** Sure.

**Me:** Just to inform you in advance, this interview will be recorded. Starting off, would you be interested in using a tool that shows you how each course is connected to specific skills and career paths?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely.

**Me:** What do you think would make this tool more appealing for you?

**Interviewee:** If the description is clear and it requires fewer steps for me to get the results that I want. So, basically, a simpler UI.

**Me:** How helpful would it be for you to know exactly which skills you will develop from each course before you enroll?

**Interviewee:** I think it is definitely very helpful because I can know the skills that I need, and then I can relate them to my future career as well.

**Me:** Would you use this tool as a potential tool to show your career path, like what path you could take based on the courses you have selected so far?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely, as long as it is clear and related to my future career.

**Me:** Do you think a tool that links your courses to your career goals would be useful? For example, if you want to work in a certain area, do you think this type of tool would help you align your courses with whatever career you want to work in the future?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely. I think it bridges the gap between my studies and my future career. I think a lot of students struggle with how to relate or apply the knowledge they gain in university to the workplace. So, if something can bridge the gap between both of these, then yes.

**Me:** Would you use it to compare different courses based on the skills they develop?

**Interviewee:** Yeah.

**Me:** How would you prioritize the features when choosing a course? What do you think is the most required feature in a tool like this? For example, what would be your most-used feature, then second, third, and so on?

**Interviewee:** Sorry, can you give me an example of a feature?

**Me:** An example of a feature could be a career path feature that tells you what skills are needed. Another feature could be listing out what skills you can learn from a course.

**Interviewee:** Okay. I think all of them are important, based on the features that you mentioned. But for me, when I am still studying, I would focus more on the skills that I can develop instead of focusing too much on a certain career pathway that I am interested in. So, I would say the skills that I can potentially develop from the course I pick would be the most important.

**Me:** How easy would it be for you to list the skills that you have developed so far from all the courses you have taken?

**Interviewee:** Do you mind repeating the question?

**Me:** Based on all the courses that you have taken so far, currently and in the past, how easy do you think it would be for you to list out all the skills? For example, for me, I am in Commerce, so I can list out that I have done coding, different languages, and other things. Do you think a tool that helps map out what skills you have developed would make you feel more confident, or do you think you can do it without the tool?

**Interviewee:** I think the tool would definitely be helpful if it breaks things down in more detail. When you enroll in a course at university, usually the description is quite broad. For example, if I take developmental psychology, they may just say that I will understand the development of human beings, but it does not really break down each section or each week in detail.

**Me:** That makes sense. Do you feel confident that the courses you have taken or are taking now will lead you to your desired career?

**Interviewee:** Honestly, I am not really confident. I think the courses are very broad. When they teach, I believe it opens a very broad pathway, so I do not think it leads to a specific career pathway.

**Me:** Think about a course that you took recently where you were unsure whether the elective would be a good choice or not. Do you think if we had a tool showing skills and career paths, it would have helped you make a better decision?

**Interviewee:** I think definitely, because usually when they list out the course description, it only explains what you are going to learn. But they do not mention what skills you are going to develop from that course. I think those are two very different things. So, listing out the skills that can potentially be developed is much more helpful than just knowing what the course is about.

**Me:** Lastly, if this tool existed, do you think you would use it every time you needed to choose a course, like every semester during course picking?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely. Especially for electives, because they are not compulsory courses. I have more options to pick from, and if there is an opportunity for me to use this tool, why not?

**Me:** Would you recommend it to others?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely. Based on the conversation that we had, I feel like it is something helpful, even though I have never used it before.

**Me:** Thank you so much for your time. That is it from me.

**Interviewee:** You’re welcome. Thank you.
